Questions about sexual wellbeing can be difficult to raise. If confidence, performance or body concerns are weighing on you, begin by describing what you would like to understand.

## Know what is being offered

Dr Pamba's practice refers to traditional herbs associated with men's wellbeing. A traditional belief or personal account is not evidence that a product improves sexual function or changes body size. Ask what a proposed product contains before considering its use.

## Put health questions to a clinician

A qualified health professional can assess symptoms and explain suitable options. Speak with one before taking herbal products, particularly if you use medication. Spiritual consultation does not diagnose or treat a sexual health condition.
