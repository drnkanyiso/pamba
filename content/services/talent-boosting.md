Whether your work happens on a stage, a playing field or in a studio, developing a talent can involve both excitement and uncertainty. You might be preparing for an opportunity, struggling with confidence or deciding how seriously to pursue your craft.

## Speak about what you are building

Dr Pamba's traditional practice includes blessings, prayer and symbolic items associated with personal ambition. A consultation lets you discuss how these practices relate to your own beliefs and goals.

## Give your ability room to grow

Consistent practice, coaching, constructive feedback and access to opportunities shape progress. Spiritual support may be part of your personal routine, but it does not promise recognition, selection or success.
