You may be putting in the effort while wondering when your work will be recognised. A missed promotion, changing role or uncertain job situation can raise questions about where to go next.

## Bring your ambitions into focus

Discuss your workplace concerns and the opportunities you hope to pursue. Dr Pamba offers prayer and traditional rituals as spiritual support for people considering advancement, greater responsibility or a fresh direction.

## Pair reflection with action

Consider the feedback, skills and conversations that could help you move forward. Hiring and promotion remain decisions made by employers; spiritual practice cannot secure a position, salary increase or contract.
