A disappointing result can be discouraging, especially when effort has already gone into learning. Adults and parents or guardians can discuss their educational concerns with Dr Pamba as part of a traditional spiritual consultation.

## Look beyond a single result

Explain the learning situation, the challenges you have noticed and the aspirations behind them. Dr Pamba can describe the prayers, charms and symbolic items associated with education in his practice. Consultations are for adults; parents or guardians can ask about their family concerns.

## Keep learning support at the centre

Study routines, attendance, teaching and individual learning needs all matter. Work with educators and learning specialists where appropriate. Spiritual practices cannot guarantee grades, admission or examination success.
